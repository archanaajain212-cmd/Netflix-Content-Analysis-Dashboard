NETFLIX CONTENT ANALYSIS
INSIGHTS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
This project analyzes Netflix movies and TV shows to understand the distribution of content across countries, genres, ratings, and release years. The project uses Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI to explore the dataset, identify patterns, and present meaningful insights through an interactive dashboard.

2. KEY AREAS OF ANALYSIS
Distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
Top 10 Countries by Netflix Content.
Most Common Genres on Netflix.
Content Ratings Distribution.
Content Released by Year.
Netflix Content Added by Year.
Movie Duration Analysis.
Comparison of Movies and TV Shows by Release Year.

3. KEY INSIGHTS
Content Type Analysis: Compares the number of movies and TV shows available in the dataset.
Country-wise Analysis: Identifies the countries most frequently represented in the Netflix content catalogue.
Genre Analysis: Highlights the most common genres available on Netflix.
Content Ratings: Examines the distribution of titles across different content ratings.
Release Year Trends: Analyzes the distribution of titles by their original release year.
Content Addition Trends: Examines how the number of titles added to Netflix varies over time.
Movie Duration Analysis: Studies the duration of movies to understand the distribution of their running times.

5. DATA LIMITATIONS
The dataset contains information about Netflix titles, including their type, country, genre, rating, release year, and duration.
Some columns may contain missing values.
Certain titles may be associated with multiple countries or genres.
The dataset does not directly provide viewing figures, audience preferences, revenue, or popularity.
The analysis describes patterns in the available catalogue and cannot independently explain why particular titles perform better.

6. CONCLUSION
The Netflix Content Analysis project demonstrates how data analytics tools can be used to explore and understand a streaming platform's content catalogue. Using Excel, SQL, and Python, the project examines content types, genres, countries, ratings, release years, and movie durations. Power BI presents these findings through an interactive dashboard, making the results easier to understand. Overall, the project helps identify content distribution patterns and provides a foundation for further analysis and data-driven content planning.
